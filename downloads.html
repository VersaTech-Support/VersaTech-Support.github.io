Setup Guide

How to Install

Follow these three simple steps to get NamStudy Prep running on your Android device.

1

Download the APK

Tap the download button above to save the .apk file to your device. You can use any browser.

2

Allow Unknown Sources

Go to Settings → Security and enable "Install from Unknown Sources" for your browser app.

3

Install & Launch

Open the downloaded file and tap "Install". Once complete, launch NamStudy Prep from your home screen.
