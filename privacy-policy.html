Privacy Policy

How NamStudy Prep handles and protects your information.

📅 Effective Date: 20 August 2026 🔄 Last Updated: 20 August 2026

NamStudy Prep is an educational study and examination-preparation application provided by VersaTech Support ("we", "us", or "our").

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information associated with your use of NamStudy Prep. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, how it is protected, and the choices available to you.

1 Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create or use a NamStudy Prep account, we may collect information such as:

  • Email address
  • Name or display name
  • User role
  • School or educational institution
  • Profile information you choose to provide
  • Profile photograph or avatar, where you choose to upload one

This information is used to create, authenticate, maintain, and personalize your account.

1.2 Educational and Application Information

Depending on how you use NamStudy Prep, information associated with your account may include:

  • Selected school
  • Subjects or educational areas associated with your account
  • Access to study and examination resources
  • Information required to provide application features
  • Other information you voluntarily submit through the application

This information is used to provide relevant educational content and functionality.

1.3 Subscription Information

NamStudy Prep may offer optional paid subscription features, including NamStudy Prep VIP.

Subscriptions are purchased through the applicable app-store billing system and may be managed using RevenueCat.

We may process or receive information necessary to determine whether your account has an active subscription and to provide the corresponding features.

We do not store your full credit-card or debit-card details. Payment information is handled by the applicable app store and its payment systems.

1.4 Technical Information

When you use NamStudy Prep, limited technical information may be processed as necessary to operate, secure, maintain, and troubleshoot the application.

This may include information such as:

  • Application version
  • Operating-system information
  • Device or platform information
  • Technical diagnostics
  • Crash or error information

This information is used to maintain application reliability, investigate technical problems, and improve the service.

2 How We Use Information

We may use information associated with your use of NamStudy Prep to:

  • Create and manage accounts
  • Authenticate users and maintain secure sessions
  • Provide educational resources and application functionality
  • Connect users with their selected school or educational environment
  • Provide profile and account features
  • Manage subscription access and entitlements
  • Provide customer and technical support
  • Maintain application security
  • Detect and prevent unauthorized use or abuse
  • Diagnose crashes, errors, and technical issues
  • Improve and develop NamStudy Prep
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements
🛡️

We do not sell your personal information.

3 Third-Party Service Providers

NamStudy Prep uses third-party services to support portions of the application.

Supabase

NamStudy Prep uses Supabase for services that may include authentication, database functionality, storage, and related backend infrastructure.

Information processed through Supabase may include account information, profile information, application data, and other information necessary to provide the service.

RevenueCat

NamStudy Prep uses RevenueCat to manage subscription information and subscription entitlements.

RevenueCat may process information necessary to associate subscription purchases with an application user and determine the user's subscription status.

Google Play

The Android version of NamStudy Prep is distributed through Google Play.

Purchases made through Google Play are processed through Google's applicable billing systems. Google may independently collect and process information in accordance with its own privacy policies and terms.

Third-party providers process information according to their own applicable terms and privacy policies.

4 Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information associated with NamStudy Prep from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Information may be stored using secure cloud infrastructure provided by third-party service providers.

Although reasonable safeguards are used, no electronic storage system or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5 Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide NamStudy Prep and its features
  • Maintain user accounts
  • Provide customer support
  • Maintain security and system integrity
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Resolve disputes
  • Enforce applicable agreements

When information is no longer required, it may be deleted or anonymized, subject to applicable legal and legitimate business requirements.

6 Account and Personal Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their NamStudy Prep account and associated personal information.

To request account or personal data deletion, contact us at:

sversatech@gmail.com

When a valid deletion request is received, we will take reasonable steps to delete information associated with the account, except where certain information is required to be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, financial, or other legitimate obligations.

⚠️

Deleting an account does not necessarily cancel an active subscription. Subscription cancellation must be performed through the applicable app-store subscription controls.

7 Children's Privacy

NamStudy Prep is an educational application intended to support students and other users of educational services.

We aim to handle personal information responsibly and in accordance with applicable privacy and child-protection laws.

Where users are minors, use of the application may be subject to the involvement, permission, or supervision of a parent, guardian, school, or other responsible adult, where required by applicable law.

If you believe that personal information belonging to a child has been collected inappropriately, please contact us at sversatech@gmail.com so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

8 Third-Party Websites and Services

NamStudy Prep may provide access to or reference educational resources, websites, or services operated by third parties.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices or security of third-party services. Users should review the privacy policies of those services before providing personal information.

9 Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of personal information
  • Request restriction of certain processing
  • Object to certain processing activities
  • Withdraw consent where applicable

Requests may be submitted by contacting:

sversatech@gmail.com

We may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests in order to protect your account and information.

10 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to NamStudy Prep, our services, technology, or applicable legal requirements.

When changes are made, the Last Updated date at the beginning of this policy will be updated.

Where appropriate, significant changes may also be communicated through the application or other available means.
